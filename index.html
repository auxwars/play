The Setup

1

Add all players on the Home screen. You need at least 2, but it's way more fun with 4+.

2

Pick a wheel theme — each wheel has a different vibe. Or go full chaos with All Categories.

3

Set how many points it takes to win. 5 is quick, 10 is a full night.

Each Round

4

One player is the Judge 🎤 for the round. They don't submit a song — they decide.

5

Hit Spin. Whatever category it lands on is the prompt for the round.

6

Every non-judge player picks a real song for that category. Name it out loud or show it to the judge.

7

The judge picks their favorite. Tap that player's name to award the point. The judge rotates every round.

Special Rounds

⚡ Double Points — There's a 20% chance any spin triggers double points. Win this round and get 2 points instead of 1.

🔥 Hot Streak — Win 3+ rounds in a row and fire emojis stack up next to your name. Pure psychological warfare.

House Rules (Optional)

No repeats — If someone already played a song earlier in the night, it's dead. Pick something else.

30-second rule — Everyone must name their song within 30 seconds or they're out for that round.

Judge's veto — Once per game, the judge can declare no winner and spin again. Use it wisely.

Custom Wheels — Hit the Create tab to build your own wheel with inside jokes, specific genres, or anything you want.
